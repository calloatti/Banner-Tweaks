Adds clean subfolders and pagination for Timberborn decals, supporting the Moddable Decal Groups standard. Easily browse custom designs by mod and group in an expanded grid view.

### Overview
Banner Tweaks completely overhauls the in-game banner and tail pattern selection menu. Automatically organizes your banners and tail patterns into clean, structured subfolders and mod groups.

### How to use
Click the Browse button in the building GUI, or double click the building (Pole Banner, Square Banner, Detailer). Click Banner/Tail to select, press ESC or click the red X to close, or double click Banner/Tail to select and close the window.

### Key Features
Adds support for folders in the Banners and Tails folders.
Native compatibility with the Moddable Decal Groups mod standard, automatically loading custom group specifications defined by mod creators.

### NEW: Borderless Square Banner
Add a new building similar to the Square Banner but borderless.   

### Folder & Group Navigation
Mod Containers ([MOD]): Automatically gathers custom patterns introduced by mods into their own dedicated group.   
Custom Groups ([GROUP]): Sub-categorizes patterns into custom subgroups (e.g., Consumables, Ingredients, Fluids) as defined by mod authors.
Directory Folders ([FOLDER]): Seamlessly navigates disk subfolder structures.

### Enhanced Grid UI
Expanded 10x5 grid view displaying up to 47 items per page.   
Navigation controls including Top, Prev, and Next buttons alongside mouse wheel scrolling support.   
Clear title header tracking your active category and folder depth (e.g., Banners\Banner Goods\Goods: Consumables).

### Comparison Slots
Dedicated Original slot to remember what pattern was placed before you started browsing.   
Live Current slot showing your newly selected pattern in real-time.
